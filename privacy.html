Privacy Notice.

How 333 Systems Ltd, trading as 333 Software, handles information when you visit this website or contact us.

Who operates this website

The operator is 333 Systems Ltd (company number 16844093), registered in England and Wales and trading as 333 Software. Its registered office is 10 Charles Close, Winchester, England, SO23 7HT.

Website hosting and technical information

This review version of the website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub says it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. Your browser and network may also exchange technical information needed to deliver a webpage. See GitHub Pages information and the GitHub Privacy Statement for GitHub's handling of that information.

When you contact us

This website provides email links and does not have a contact form or account registration. If you email us, your message, sender address and message details are processed by your email provider and the services used to deliver and host the message. When an enquiry reaches us, 333 Systems Ltd uses it to respond. The public 333 Software contact address is contact@333software.co.uk; the mailbox is hosted by Namecheap Private Email. For parent-company matters you can also contact 333 Systems Ltd at contact@333systemsltd.co.uk.

Purposes and lawful bases

For routine enquiries, 333 Systems Ltd relies on its legitimate interests in responding to communications you choose to send and managing the related discussion. If you ask us to take steps before entering a contract, or an existing contract applies, we process relevant information as necessary for those steps or the contract. We retain accounting and tax records where required to meet legal obligations. GitHub separately logs visitor IP addresses for its own security purposes; see GitHub's privacy information for its processing and lawful bases.

Service providers and international processing

GitHub provides hosting for this review website and Namecheap hosts the public 333 Software mailbox. GitHub says it processes personal data in multiple locations, including the United States, and generally relies on Standard Contractual Clauses for certain transfers from the UK. Namecheap's published Data Processing Addendum permits US processing and includes UK transfer terms where applicable. Whether those terms cover this mailbox depends on the service and account terms; the region used for this mailbox is not specified here. Please refer to the GitHub Privacy Statement and Namecheap Privacy Policy for further information about their processing.

Retention

General enquiries are retained for up to 12 months after the last meaningful correspondence. Active support and customer matters are kept while needed, then reviewed according to record type. Accounting and tax records are normally retained for six years from the end of the relevant company financial year. We review correspondence periodically and delete personal information no longer required. We retain information longer only where necessary for legal obligations, disputes or other justified purposes. GitHub determines retention for its own security logs under its published policies.

Cookies and analytics

The current website code does not include Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, advertising pixels or an analytics consent tool. This describes the site code; it does not rule out technical processing by the hosting provider. No optional analytics are currently configured by 333 Software on this website.

Your rights and complaints

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct or erase your personal information, restrict processing, object to processing based on our legitimate interests, or receive information you provided in a portable form. Contact us to make a request. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

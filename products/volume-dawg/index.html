Why it exists

Listening levels should not demand constant attention.

Repeatedly adjusting the volume interrupts listening. It often starts when sources vary, an advert or content change jumps in level, dialogue competes with louder sounds, or quiet listening makes soft and loud material difficult to balance.

Volume DAWg Standard combines level control, independent processing tools and simple tonal adjustment while leaving you in charge of each setting. Results still depend on the source and your chosen controls.

01

Uneven sources

Different clips, speakers and videos arrive at noticeably different listening levels.

02

Sudden level jumps

An inserted advert or abrupt content change can suddenly sound louder than the surrounding material.

03

Dialogue vs louder material

Speech can be difficult to follow against engines, music, effects or other dominant sounds.

04

Wide dynamics / quiet listening

Listening quietly can mean choosing between hearing soft material and containing loud sections.

Verified capabilities

One control surface. Five clear layers.

The Standard interface brings its main processing, tuning, routing and monitoring tools into one Windows desktop interface. Film, Online and Punch change Smart Compression behaviour; Dynamic Levelling, Ad Spike Control and Dialogue Enhancement remain independently controllable.

01 / Control

Shape level changes

  • Dynamic LevellingIndependently controls sustained differences in listening level.
  • Smart CompressionControls dynamics with adjustable strength and three dedicated compression presets.
  • Ad Spike ControlIndependently reduces the impact of sudden loud or spike-like sections.
  • Night ModeSupports controlled playback where large level changes are undesirable, including quieter listening.

02 / Smart Compression presets

Choose compression behaviour

  • FilmA Smart Compression preset for film and TV listening.
  • OnlineA Smart Compression preset for everyday online video.
  • PunchA Smart Compression preset with a more assertive response.

03 / Tuning

Fine-tune the chain

  • Dialogue EnhancementIndependently adjusts processing intended to improve dialogue intelligibility.
  • Input TrimAdjusts the signal level entering the processing chain.
  • 3-band EQProvides straightforward tonal adjustment without a full parametric-EQ workflow.
  • LimiterForms part of the processed-output protection.

04 / Routing

Choose the signal path

  • Application routingSelect a Windows application or source through Settings for processed playback.
  • Latency profilesBalance playback responsiveness and stability separately from audio processing controls.
  • Guided setupVolume DAWg guides the user into setup when selected-application routing requires attention.

05 / Monitoring

See what the processing is doing

  • Level metersRead input, output and Smart Compression gain reduction in real time.
  • Signal viewsInspect the analyser, spectrum and waveform while audio is playing.
  • Meter responseChoose Fast, Balanced or Smooth visual response independently of playback latency profiles.

Platform & processing

Control your listening.

You can't control how every source is mixed. You can improve how you listen to it.

Volume DAWg processes audio from your selected Windows application locally, helping manage differences in listening level without relying on every video, website or source to be consistently mixed.

Volume DAWg Standard is a Windows desktop application built with WPF on .NET 8. Its primary audio processing and real-time visual feedback run locally on the Windows PC.

Choose a supported Windows application and audio output in Settings. Volume DAWg routes that playback through its processing engine, with guided setup when routing needs attention.

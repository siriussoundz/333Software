# Legitimate interests assessment — routine enquiries

**Status:** Owner-review draft. This records the reasoning for review; it is not a legal certification.

## Scope

Routine enquiries voluntarily sent to 333 Software by email, including the sender's address and the information in the message. This assessment does not cover marketing, profiling, or GitHub's separate processing of visitor IP addresses for hosting security.

## Purpose test

The purpose is to read and respond to an enquiry and manage the resulting correspondence. This is a genuine business purpose connected to a communication initiated by the sender.

## Necessity test

Using the sender address and relevant message content is necessary to understand the enquiry and reply. Information not needed for that purpose should not be requested or retained for it.

## Balancing test

A person who emails the business would reasonably expect their message to be used to reply and handle the discussion. Routine correspondence is ordinarily low impact, but free-text messages can contain sensitive details or information about other people. The approved retention policy limits general enquiries to up to 12 months after the last meaningful correspondence, with periodic review and deletion when no longer needed. Active customer/support matters and legally required accounting or tax records follow their separately stated retention rules.

Before relying on this basis, the owner should confirm that actual mailbox access and handling are limited to people who need them, that enquiry details are not reused for unrelated purposes, and that requests containing unnecessary sensitive or third-party information are handled appropriately.

## Provisional conclusion and related bases

Legitimate interests appears a suitable provisional basis for routine enquiries, subject to owner review and confirmation of the handling described above. Where a person asks for pre-contract steps, or processing is necessary for an existing contract, the relevant contractual basis may apply instead. Legal obligations may apply to accounting and tax records where retention is required by law.

This assessment does not cover GitHub's security logging. Confirm whether 333 Systems Ltd receives or uses those logs; if it does, assess its own purpose and basis separately rather than relying on GitHub's stated purposes.
